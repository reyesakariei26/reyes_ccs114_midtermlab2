ABOUT TINTA ᜐᜒᜈᜒᜅ᜔

A Canvas of Filipino Identity.

TINTA is a student-created digital gallery that celebrates the creativity and artistic heritage of Filipinos.

Filipino art carries stories about history, family, community, nature, traditions, struggles, and everyday life.

Through this gallery, these works are presented in a simple and responsive digital space.

TINTA

Filipino art preserves stories, traditions, experiences, and cultural identity across generations.

Filipino art history includes artists such as Juan Luna, Fernando Amorsolo, Carlos "Botong" Francisco, Anita Magsaysay-Ho, and many other artists.

"Tinta" means ink in Filipino. The name represents the idea of creating stories through lines, colors, and artistic expression.
